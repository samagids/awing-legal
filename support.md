## Common questions

### Why does a word show a microphone instead of a speaker?

Because nobody has recorded that word yet. The app plays only recordings
made by human Awing speakers — it never synthesises Awing, because no
text-to-speech voice can produce Awing tone correctly, and a wrong tone
teaches a child the wrong word.

Tapping the microphone opens the recorder with that word already selected.
If you speak Awing, that is how the gap gets closed.

### Why do some words have no picture?

Some dictionary entries have no honest picture — grammar words, markers,
and entries that name one specific local group or place. A blank card is
deliberate there. A confidently wrong picture is worse than none.

### Do I need an account?

No. Every lesson, quiz and game works fully offline with no account. Signing
in with Google is optional and only adds progress sync across your devices.

### How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device. If you signed
in with Google and want your cloud data deleted as well, email
[samagids@gmail.com](mailto:samagids@gmail.com) and we will delete it.

### I found a wrong word, spelling or translation

Please tell us. Use **Contribute** in the app, or email us. Awing
orthography decisions are made from the published sources and by Awing
speakers, not guessed, so corrections from speakers are genuinely useful.

### Is there anything to buy in the app?

No. The app is free, has no ads, no in-app purchases and no premium tier.
