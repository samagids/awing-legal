# Awing Learning

An offline-first app for learning the **Awing** language (Northwest Region,
Cameroon), built for children and beginners by Dr. Guidion Sama, DIT.

- [Privacy Policy](privacy)
- [Support](support)

Every word of Awing audio in the app is a recording made by a human Awing
speaker. The app does not synthesise Awing speech.
